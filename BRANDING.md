# Branding and official support

Forks, code reuse and contributions are welcome. Independent projects should use their own project name, logo, artwork and package identity. We do not authorize ROMarr branding as the identity of another product or distribution.

Credit ROMarr as upstream, preserve the required MIT copyright and license notices, identify your own maintainer and provide your own support links. Attribution and accurate compatibility references are welcome. This policy does not change the MIT license.

The [official ROMarr listing](https://ca.unraid.net/apps/romarr-04jslct0riva81?q=romarr) uses `ghcr.io/blizzhacker/romarr:latest` and [our template](templates/romarr.xml).

The [ROMarrNG listing](https://ca.unraid.net/apps/romarrng-0ix7ta406cq2i0?q=romarr) uses `ghcr.io/snapetech-org/romarrng:latest` and its own template. ROMarrNG is independently maintained. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or supported by the ROMarr maintainers.

See the [upstream official-distribution policy](https://github.com/BlizzHacker/romarr/blob/main/docs/OFFICIAL-DISTRIBUTION.md) for supported sources and how to identify an installation.
